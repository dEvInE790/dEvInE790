How it works

From your idea to finished furniture

  1. Discuss your idea

    Send us a message with what you have in mind, plus photos or a sketch if you have them.

  2. Measure & design

    We take exact measurements and agree the design, materials and finish with you.

  3. Build

    Your piece is made to measure in our workshop.

  4. Install & complete

    We deliver, fit and finish everything, and leave your space clean.
