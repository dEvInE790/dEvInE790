About Us

Crafting Imagination Into Reality

Odinga Arts & Sculpture is a creative art and sculpture business founded and owned by Odilli Gabriel, whose roots are in Ndokwa East Local Government Area of Delta State. From our base in Asaba, we turn ideas into finished work — sculpted wall reliefs, statues, painted murals and the architectural details that give a building its character.

Every piece begins with a conversation. Whether you are finishing a home, brightening a school, shaping the entrance to an estate or giving a business space a signature feature, we listen to what you imagine and work to bring it into being with care, skill and a strong eye for detail.

Why Choose Odinga

Made with care, from first sketch to final finish

We believe good art and architectural detail should feel personal. Here is what guides every commission we take on.

  1. Creative & Custom Designs

    Every piece is designed around your idea and your space — not copied from a catalogue.

  2. Attention to Detail

    Proportion, texture and finish are considered closely, because the small details are what people notice.

  3. Artistic Craftsmanship

    Hand-modelled relief, sculpture and painting, carried out with patience and skill.

  4. Architectural Decoration

    Pillars, window hoods, flower caps and fence designs that complement the architecture of your building.

  5. Customer-Focused Service

    Clear communication from first enquiry to completion, so you know what to expect at every stage.
