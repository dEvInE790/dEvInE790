I can’t change the past, and I know that saying ‘I’m sorry’ doesn’t automatically heal the hurt. I also understand why you might feel the way you do about me now.

I’ve been reflecting on myself, and I want to become someone who treats the people he loves with the respect they deserve. I know that real change isn’t something I can prove with a website or a few words. It has to show in my actions.

I’m truly sorry for the hurt I caused you. You don’t owe me forgiveness, and you don’t owe me another chance. I just wanted you to know that I recognize my mistakes and that I’m sorry.
