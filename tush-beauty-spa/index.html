Our Services

Premium Skincare Treatments

Tailored facial treatments for healthy, radiant and confident skin — designed with Black and melanin-rich skin in mind. Every visit begins with a conversation about your skin, so your treatment can be personalised to you.

For pricing, availability and advice on which treatment is right for you, call 346-315-5575 or 346-822-3348.

01

Deep Cleansing Facials

A thorough, purifying facial that cleanses, gently exfoliates and decongests — leaving skin feeling fresh, clean and balanced.

Our Deep Cleansing Facial is a reset for your skin. It focuses on lifting away the build-up of everyday life — makeup, oil and dullness — so your skin feels clean, refreshed and comfortable.

A good fit if your skin feels congested, tired or simply in need of a fresh start.

Book Now
02

Acne Facial

A focused facial for breakout-prone skin, designed to deep-clean pores and help skin feel calmer and look clearer.

Our Acne Facial is designed for skin that's prone to congestion and breakouts. The treatment concentrates on thorough cleansing and calming care, with the aim of helping your skin feel more balanced and look clearer.

Good to know: if you're currently using prescription skincare or have a skin condition, please mention it when you book so we can advise you.

Book Now
03

Glowing Facial

A radiance-boosting treatment that refreshes tired-looking skin and brings out your natural, healthy-looking glow.

The Glowing Facial is all about radiance. It's designed to refresh dull, tired-looking skin and leave your complexion looking luminous, healthy and glowing — perfect before a special occasion or simply as a treat for yourself.

Book Now
04

Deena Planning

A gentle skin-refining treatment for a smoother-looking surface and a soft, polished finish.

A gentle refining treatment focused on a smoother-looking skin surface and a soft, polished finish that feels lovely under makeup — or on its own.

Want to know more? Call us and we'll happily explain exactly what this treatment involves and whether it's right for your skin.

Book Now
05

Anti-Aging

A nourishing facial focused on the visible signs of aging, designed to help skin look smoother, firmer and refreshed.

Our Anti-Aging treatment is a nourishing, restorative facial that focuses on the visible signs of aging — helping your skin look smoother, firmer and more refreshed, while giving you a deeply relaxing moment of self-care.

Book Now
06

Whitening Facials

A brightening facial focused on a more even-looking, luminous complexion and a fresh, radiant finish.

Our Whitening Facial is a brightening treatment focused on a more even-looking, luminous complexion. Ask us about it when you book — we'll talk through your skin goals and what you can expect.

Book Now
07

HydraFacial

A multi-step hydrating facial that cleanses, exfoliates and infuses skin with moisture for a fresh, dewy finish.

HydraFacial is a multi-step treatment that cleanses, exfoliates and deeply hydrates in one relaxing session. It's a popular choice for anyone who wants fresh, plump-looking, dewy skin.

Book Now

About Us

Where Skincare Meets Self-Care.

At Tush Beauty Spa Houston, we believe healthy skin is more than a routine — it's a form of self-care. Every visit is a moment to pause, breathe and be looked after.

We focus on caring for Black and melanin-rich skin, and every treatment is chosen with its unique needs in mind. Our treatments are personalised to you. We take the time to understand your skin and what you'd like to achieve, then tailor your facial so you leave feeling refreshed, restored and confident in your own skin. Because you deserve to feel beautiful — always.

  • i.

    Care for Melanin-Rich Skin

    Treatments tailored to Black skin, your skin type and your goals — never one-size-fits-all.

  • ii.

    Professional Treatments

    A full menu of facials, from deep cleansing to HydraFacial, under one roof.

  • iii.

    Confidence, Always

    Our goal is simple: helping you feel confident and comfortable in your skin.
