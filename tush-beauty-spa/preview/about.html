About us

Beauty, as a ritual

Beauty care should feel personal, unhurried and kind. That belief shapes everything at Tush Beauty Spa, from how we welcome you to how we take care of you during your visit.

01

Our philosophy

We see beauty as a ritual rather than a rush. Small moments, like a warm welcome, a clear conversation about what you would like, and time to settle in, make the difference between an appointment and an experience you look forward to.

02

The atmosphere

Our space is designed to help you slow down: soft light, warm neutral tones and a quiet, comfortable setting where you can switch off for a while.

03

Our commitment to you

We take time to understand what you are looking for and talk you through your treatment, so you always know what to expect. Your comfort, your questions and your time are always respected.

Why clients choose us

Thoughtful care, from hello to goodbye

  • Personal, unhurried care

  • A calm, welcoming space
