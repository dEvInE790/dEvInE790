About Tush Beauty Spa

A quieter, more personal kind of beauty appointment

Tush Beauty Spa was created for people who want their time in the chair to feel like their own. Every appointment begins with listening: what you would like, how you like to feel, and how much time you have.

From there, your treatment is shaped around you, in a calm space where you can slow down, be looked after, and leave feeling like yourself, only more rested.
